## 1. Problem

The impl-review PRD (merged, #407) binds *what* the reviewer must produce — schema'd findings kin to codegate's core (FR-5), persisted machine-harvestable-without-free-text on the proposal (FR-6/FR-13), dispositions bound to their author's identity (FR-16), a verdict bound to the reviewed revision (UB-6), all from four fresh-context lenses (FR-2/FR-3) at bounded cost (NFR-1) — and its four open questions deferred the schema, the measurement home, the runtime, and the deep-band dial to this design. Implementation cannot start without them, and the direction was grilled and gated in a real stage-3 session (the record this design is fed from).

The moment is specific: the two sibling reviewers shipped typed-markdown reports and *parked* JSON-native mechanization "for the engine era, when a mechanical consumer of findings and measurements exists" (prd-review-artifacts §2). That consumer now exists — impl-review's own measurement harvest (FR-13) and codegate's WR-2 corpus trigger. This feature is that graduation, for its own class: a machine reads these findings.

## 2. Decision

**Decision:** impl-review realizes as a **machine-native payload with a rendered human face**, persisted **on the proposal's own medium as one self-contained artifact — one source of truth, never split** (`design-brainstorm:impl-review-tech-design#D-1`, `#D-5`): one closed, versioned **`impl-review-report/v1`** schema — **grammar-compatible** with codegate's `Finding` (byte-compatible overlapping fields, a compatibility test not a Go import — NR-1) plus review-only fields — carrying a machine payload and a rendering derived from it. **A proposal lives on exactly one medium** (the code-host it was raised on); the persistence port has two adapters (Carina, GitHub) because pyxis supports two code-hosts, and a given proposal uses **one** — there is no cross-platform mirroring and no splitting of a payload across containers. The payload stays **compact by prompt discipline** (§3.5), so it fits any medium as one artifact; verbosity is a skill defect prevented at the source, never a persistence case. Measurement is a **checkpoint harvest parsing those payloads directly** (`#D-2`). The reviewer runs as the **`/impl-review` skill spawning four fresh-context subagents — sibling to the implementing session, not children of it — seeded from one review context pack** (`#D-3`, reusing `context-pack-v1`'s machinery over its existing admitted kinds). The deep-band second-instance verify is a **dormant, data-gated seam** (`#D-4`). The design **emits** the downgrade verdict; **consuming** it into the merge lane's `Downgrader` seam is an owed lane amendment (§7 Q4).

Why it is safe: the payload is the single record every consumer reads (harvest, human rendering, downstream — AP-21); it is grammar-compatible with codegate's core (one family for the host's fixer) without coupling to its struct (NR-1); the **per-round** reviewed-revision field closes the AP-13 staleness seam across the two-round model (UB-6); the shared pack cuts the dominant cost without sharing judgment (finding-blind lenses — FR-3); and the sibling-session spawn is what actually makes the review independent of a possibly-compromised implementer (FR-2).

## 3. The design

### 3.1 The `impl-review-report/v1` schema

Closed and versioned (`impl-review-report/v1`); additive within v1, breaking = v2 by supersession. Machine-native (JSON), the graduation the record classes deferred.

**The finding — grammar compatibility, not struct embedding (NR-1).** impl-review declares its **own** finding type whose overlapping fields are **byte-compatible with codegate's `Finding` grammar** — `path`, `line`, `message`, `log_ref` (same JSON keys, same meaning) — so the host's fixer parses codegate and impl-review findings as **one grammar family**; plus the review-only fields: `rule_id` (the lens+rule identity — its own field, never an overload of codegate's `check_id`), `lens` (spec-fidelity · design-conformance · security-abuse · correctness-critic), `severity` (blocking · advisory), `cal_row` (the calibration citation, FR-12), `acceptance`, and `disposition`. A **compatibility test** (R4) asserts the overlapping keys match codegate's current grammar; there is no Go import of codegate's struct and no dead field (codegate's `check_id`/`rung`/`class` are not carried — impl-review has no rungs and its own `rule_id`/`lens` are the identity). This is D-1's "one grammar family, kin to codegate's core" realized as wire-compatibility — more robust than struct coupling, and zero codegate change by construction (NR-1, DINV-6).

**The disposition object (attribution, FR-16).** Each disposition carries `actor` (worker · operator), `verdict` (applied · declined · accepted · rejected), `reason` (mandatory on `declined`), and `attribution` — **the medium author of the disposition artifact** (§3.2): a worker disposition is authored by the worker in the payload; an **operator disposition is a separate operator-authored artifact** whose medium author *is* the operator (a Carina decision-record author, a GitHub PR review/comment author), never a field a worker could write. The harvest counts an operator-class disposition only when its `attribution` resolves to the operator identity (§3.3); a mismatch is dropped (FR-16, DINV-4).

**The report envelope and its rounds.** One payload per proposal per review. Envelope-level: `band` (skip · standard · deep, from the lane's class — FR-9) and the live `verdict` (approve · approve-with-advisories · request-changes · escalate — the downgrade-only set, FR-7). **Per-round** (the two-round cap structural, each round a distinct evaluation): the round's **`reviewed_revision`** (the head sha *that round* evaluated — the UB-6/AP-13 binding; a fix between rounds is a new sha, so each round binds its own), the round's **`context_id`** + provenance (model, prompts version, cal version — the fresh-instance identity, FR-2/NFR-3), the `FND-n` finding set, and the EVT counts (findings/candidates/killed). The live verdict's staleness (DINV-7) is evaluated against the **latest round's** `reviewed_revision`. The **escalation brief** (present iff `escalate` — the single conflict, options, decision needed; FR-17, embedded). `FND-n` dense per report across rounds; cited as `impl-review:<proposal-ref>#FND-3`.

### 3.2 The persistence port and its two adapters

**Decision:** persistence is a **port** — `Persist(payload) → ref` and `Read(ref) → payload` — with two adapters, one per supported code-host; a proposal's review lives on **that proposal's own medium** as **one self-contained artifact** — the single source of truth for the review, never mirrored to the other medium and never split across containers (`#D-5`). The payload is written once; the human report is **rendered from it**, never authored separately (foreclosing the ALT-3 divergence). Both adapters are first-class; **GitHub is built first** (operator's gate ruling) because the dogfood proposals are GitHub PRs — reviewing where the real proposals live; Carina lands later. Build order is not a quality hierarchy: each adapter carries the same one self-contained artifact per proposal.

- **GitHub adapter (v1).** One bot-owned PR **comment** carries the rendering + a fenced ` ```impl-review ` machine block (the marker-block reconciled against RW-3 as the port's honest realization for a medium lacking a native typed record — Principles). The compact payload (§3.5) fits one comment with wide margin, so the review is one artifact on the PR — no continuation-splitting (which would itself break one-source-of-truth). Because GitHub-first puts the anti-forgery mechanism on the v1 path, it is **specified here, not deferred**: an operator disposition is the operator's **own PR review or comment** — the review/comment's `user` *is* the operator, natively attributable (DINV-4); the skill reads that operator-authored artifact and folds its verdict into the payload with `attribution` referencing it — *never* an edit to the bot comment (whose per-edit authorship GitHub does not expose harvestably, the mechanism that would have collapsed). The residual integration detail (Q3) is only whether that operator-artifact reference **survives the fleet's squash-merge path** — the *mechanism* is fixed here. Write failures follow UB-5; a schema-invalid payload is voided per UB-2.
- **Carina adapter (later).** The payload is a **typed decision-record** on the work item (`local-work-tracker-v2` §3.1's structured replacement for marker grammars): the machine payload as its body, the rendering as its human region. An operator disposition is the operator's **own decision-record** on the item; the skill folds its verdict into the payload with `attribution` referencing that operator-authored record (its author *is* the operator — DINV-4). The payload remains the one review artifact; the disposition's provenance points at the operator's authored act. This adapter is the cleaner attribution surface (one record, one author) and lands after the GitHub dogfood proves the loop.

Each adapter serializes and recovers the *same* `impl-review-report/v1` bytes — one parser, so a proposal reviewed on either medium harvests identically (the port conformance suite, A-3). The human rendering is a pure function of the payload (the sibling report shape), generated, never hand-written.

### 3.3 The measurement harvest

**Decision:** measurement is a checkpoint **harvest** that **enumerates a repo's full proposal universe** (GitHub: the PR set — the v1 path, where the dogfood proposals live, its paging cost noted; Carina: a worktracker query over the proposal work items, later), **parses each `impl-review-report/v1` payload** from its medium, and computes the corpus reading **only payload fields, never rendered prose** (FR-13): coverage (M1: report-or-`skipped` present ÷ the *full* universe — the harvest is the only source that sees a never-invoked proposal, which is why it enumerates all, not just reviewed), precision (M2: operator-`accepted` ÷ operator-`disposed` blocking findings, over the disposition floor), the nit rate (operator `rejected` per review), and the miss corpus (EVT-6). Unattributable operator dispositions are excluded (§3.1/DINV-4). The `review-feedback-capture` ledger is untouched; a unified corpus, if ever wanted, is a *derived* mirror off this harvest (WR-3), never the source. M4 reads review presence + verdict from the same payloads. Steady-state: the harvest re-parses per checkpoint — O(corpus), a stated note at today's scale; incremental harvest is a future optimization.

### 3.4 The `/impl-review` skill, the review context pack, and the sibling-session topology

**Decision:** the reviewer is the **`/impl-review` skill** (the sibling-reviewer runtime), invoked by the host at the proposal boundary (FR-1). Two things the design fixes that FR-2's independence rests on:

- **The sibling-session topology.** The host spawns the reviewer as a **fresh top-level session, a sibling of the implementing worker — never a child of the worker's session** (a child would inherit the implementer's context and defeat FR-2). The four lens subagents are children of *that* reviewer root; each `context_id` derives from the reviewer root and is provenance-checked distinct from the implementing session's root (NFR-3). This is what makes "a compromised implementing agent cannot see or steer the review" true (§3.10), not subagent spawn alone (DINV-5).
- **The review context pack (T-1).** The shared read-only input assembled once — the **proposal at its reviewed revision** (the diff — mapped onto context-pack's admitted **"frozen subject under work"** kind, exactly as a PRD is prd-review's subject), the codegate verdict (part of that frozen subject), the **task charter and the governing design's binding elements** (admitted **governing-peer** kinds), and the **calibration table** (the admitted **stage-calibration** kind). No enum change is needed: every input maps onto an existing admitted kind, and — critically — **no authoring-trail kind enters** (no prior findings/dispositions; the answer-key exclusion holds, aligning with DINV-5's finding-blindness). The pack reuses `context-pack-v1`'s deterministic-projection machinery (AP-11, reuse not rebuild); the four subagents read the *same* pack — the cost move (NFR-1: one build, not four re-reads).
- **Independence (FR-2/FR-3).** The pack shares *input*, never *judgment*: each lens subagent sees the pack + its own lens instruction only, **blind to the other lenses' findings** — the finding-blindness that makes the adversarial screen real (a shared-reasoning pack would collapse four lenses into one). A screening pass filters survivors, which serialize into the payload.
- **Lifecycle (UB-8, AP-12).** A re-invocation resumes from the persisted payload's round state (authoritative there), idempotent. The **in-flight predicate** — a review with a started round and no terminal verdict past the **absent-timeout** (config data) — is what a janitor uses to distinguish a slow deep-band review from an orphan; the reaper itself executes host-side (implement-now's janitor, riding NR-2), a reasoned AP-12 deviation (Principles), but the predicate + timeout are defined here so the resource is reap-able by contract.

### 3.5 The eval suite and its protocol

**Decision:** the §5.9-basis eval families ship as fixtures beside the skill glue, versioned data (Q4). **Protocol:** each family runs **N ≥ 5 times per release** on a decode-pinned configuration recorded in the eval provenance; a family passes only if caught on **every** run (an intermittent miss is a defect, never a rerun-until-green). The floor-exclusion (EVAL-4) and injection-invariance (EVAL-5) legs are scored by a **deterministic comparator** (defect-class membership; verdict equality), not a judge model. Families grow by data PR (the miss corpus EVT-6, ABUSE-1 regression pinning); removal or threshold relaxation is a reviewed data-PR decision. `calibration.md` (`impl-review-calibration/v1`, the closed `| Row | Lens | Default severity | Escalation condition |` table) is beside the glue, cited by `cal_row`, changed by data PR. The suite is permanent CI; its runtime is budgeted as data.

**Compactness — verbosity is a source defect, not a persistence case (`#D-5`).** The skill's lens and rendering prompts bound finding and report verbosity — a finding is a few lines (the sibling reviewers' NFR-2 posture: a claim + its evidence anchor + its acceptance, not an essay), the rendering is the compact report shape — so a review payload stays small (well under any code-host's per-artifact limit) and lives as **one artifact on the proposal's medium**. A payload trending large is a prompt/verbosity defect surfaced by a **compactness eval leg** (a bound on payload size across the golden fixtures), fixed at the prompt, never worked around by splitting the artifact — splitting would break the one-source-of-truth this design rests on.

### 3.6 Boundaries, reconciliations, and non-goals

- **The merge-lane reconciliation.** The design **emits** the downgrade verdict (the persisted payload's `verdict`); **consuming** it is *not* this design's scope and *not* "zero lane-side change." merge-lane's only downgrade seam is a synchronous, diff-only `Downgrader.Downgrade(Diff)`, `nil`-by-default (WR-3-deferred). Wiring the persisted verdict in is an **owed merge-lane amendment** — a `Downgrader` adapter that reads the impl-review verdict for the proposal — aligned with merge-lane's own deferral of the model-driven downgrade stage (§7 Q4). The basis's NR-4 "existing slot, zero change" overstated this and needs a corrective amendment (the codegate-NR-2 precedent).
- **Not solved here** — the reviewer's *behavior contract* (lenses' judgment, verdict authority semantics, the ramp — the PRD binds them); codegate's finding schema (frozen; grammar-compatible, not coupled); the merge-lane consumption (owed amendment, above); the worker fix loop.
- **Owned by a future design / a later phase** — the WR-1 remote twin; the WR-3 ledger mirror; incremental harvest; codegate's WR-2 judgment rungs (fed by this corpus); the **Carina adapter** (built after the GitHub dogfood — the operator's gate ordering).
- **Implementation must not infer** — that the human report is authored (rendered from the payload — DINV-2); that a second report artifact exists (one payload — DINV-1); that a disposition's `attribution` is payload-writable (it is the disposition artifact's medium author — DINV-4); that codegate's struct is imported (grammar-compatibility, a compatibility test — DINV-6); that the pack shares judgment (input only — DINV-5); that the reviewer is a child of the implementing session (sibling — DINV-5).
- **Must remain unchanged** — codegate's grammar and ledger; the merge lane's classifier and floor; the sibling reviewers' markdown schemas (the two-grammar divergence is **accepted**: each class's harvest is its own; WR-2 consumes impl-review's JSON directly; no migration — all entities new).

### 3.7 Invariants

- **DINV-1** — one `impl-review-report/v1` payload per proposal per review; rounds append inside it; a second payload for the same proposal+review is invalid.
- **DINV-2** — the human report is a pure rendering of the payload; a hand-authored region diverging from the payload is invalid (the payload governs).
- **DINV-3** — every finding carries all review-only fields incl. a `cal_row` citation; a severity contradicting the cited row is invalid.
- **DINV-4** — an operator-class disposition's `attribution` is the medium author of a separate operator-authored disposition artifact, never a payload-writable field; an unattributable operator disposition is excluded from the corpus (FR-16).
- **DINV-5** — the reviewer runs in a session distinct from the implementing session (a sibling spawn, not a child), and the context pack is shared read-only input: a lens subagent sees the pack + its own lens instruction only, never the implementing session's context or another lens's findings (FR-2/FR-3).
- **DINV-6** — the finding schema is grammar-compatible with codegate's `Finding` (a compatibility test on the overlapping keys), not a Go import of it; no impl-review change edits codegate (NR-1).
- **DINV-7** — each round records the head sha it evaluated; the live verdict is stale iff the proposal head ≠ the latest round's `reviewed_revision`, and a stale verdict is treated as absent, never current (UB-6/AP-13).
- **DINV-8** — the harvest computes every measurement from payload fields alone over the full proposal universe; no metric reads rendered prose (FR-13/NFR-4).
- **DINV-9** — a proposal's review is one self-contained artifact on that proposal's own medium; it is never mirrored to another medium nor split across containers (one source of truth — `#D-5`). Compactness (§3.5) keeps this achievable at the source.

### 3.8 Observability

- **Events/logs** — the EVT-1..6 fields are inline in the payload (review_run/finding_persisted/disposition_recorded per round, eval_run in CI, adoption_checkpoint from the harvest, review_miss); the skill logs via `log/slog` with the `context_id`.
- **Metrics** — coverage/precision/nit/miss + M4, computed by the §3.3 harvest; the checkpoint surfaces them + the disposition-engagement signal.
- **Alerts** — none intrinsic (an invoked skill, no daemon); a stuck review is caught by the in-flight predicate + absent-timeout (§3.4) at the host janitor and surfaced at the checkpoint as a coverage/stall signal.
- **Debugging surface** — the payload is self-describing (per-round provenance, candidates/killed, reviewed revisions); "why did the reviewer miss X" reads candidates-vs-killed per lens; the harvest reconstructs the corpus from a fresh clone (NFR-4).
- **Trace/correlation** — the reviewer-root `context_id` + the host-supplied opaque invocation context thread the review to its proposal without a factory-metadata dependency (FR-2).

### 3.9 Security & privacy

Matches the header row: **yes**. (1) **model-provider egress** — the lenses are LLM calls carrying diff + charter; the same egress class as every factory LLM call (codegate, the worker), no new destination; the pack is repo-local inputs the operand owns. (2) **identity binding (FR-16)** — the disposition `attribution` is the medium author of a *separate operator-authored artifact* (Carina decision-record / GitHub PR review-or-comment), never a payload string; the harvest excludes unattributable operator dispositions, so a forged "operator accepted" (ABUSE-5) cannot poison adoption — "bot and human identities never confusable," applied to the disposition. (3) **fresh-context isolation** — the reviewer is a *sibling session* to the implementer, not a child (DINV-5), so a compromised implementing agent cannot see or steer the review from inside; A-6 fails if the reviewer inherited the implementer's context. No personal data; diffs are the operand's own code.

### 3.10 Failure modes

| Failure | System behavior | Detection | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| lens backend error/timeout past bound | bounded retry, then `review-absent` recorded on the proposal; never a silent pass (UB-1) | the runtime bound | advisory: raise flagged; post-adoption: HUMAN-route |
| schema-invalid finding/round | void the round, retry once, then record absent (UB-2) | schema validation | as UB-1 |
| persistence write failure (either adapter) | bounded retry, then absent via alternate signal (UB-5) | the write result | no silent loss or hang |
| proposal head changes post-review | the live verdict marked stale; re-review (DINV-7/UB-6) | head vs latest round's reviewed_revision | forced round 2 (advisory) / refused raise (post-adoption) |
| orphaned in-flight review (host died) | the in-flight predicate trips at the absent-timeout; the host janitor reaps + re-invokes (UB-8, AP-12 deviation) | the predicate | resume from the persisted round state |
| a review payload trends large (verbose lenses) | the compactness eval leg (§3.5) reds; the finding/rendering prompts are tightened — the payload is never split across artifacts | the compactness bound on goldens | prompt fix (a source defect, not a persistence case) |

### 3.11 Testing strategy

Seams binned per the template menu. **The persistence port** — two real adapters (Carina, GitHub) ⇒ a conformance suite every adapter passes (A-3): payload round-trip byte-identity (incl. an oversize leg on GitHub), disposition attribution recovery, rendered-face-from-payload. **The schema + harvest** — in-process/hermetic: golden payloads, the DINV-1/2/3/7/8 defect fixtures, the harvest over a mixed proposal set (incl. a never-invoked proposal, A-4). **The LLM lens backend** — true-external: injected/faked for hermetic runtime tests (the four-subagent spawn, the sibling-session A-6 leg, the finding-blindness independence test), live only in the §3.5 eval CI. **codegate compatibility** — the grammar-compatibility test (R4) pins the overlapping keys against codegate's current struct. **The merge-lane signal** — the design tests it *emits* a well-formed downgrade verdict; consumption is the owed amendment's test.

## Principles

- **AP-6 — honored.** The `impl-review-report/v1` schema is closed and machine-native — the JSON graduation the record classes staged, taken here because a machine (the harvest) is the consumer.
- **AP-10 — honored.** Measurement is code over a closed payload; the harvest computes every metric mechanically.
- **AP-11 — honored.** One finding-grammar family (codegate's core, grammar-compatible not forked); one risk predicate (the lane's classes, read); one calibration home; one context-pack machinery (reused over its existing admitted kinds, no enum change).
- **AP-12 — deviation, with reason.** The in-flight review is a throwaway resource; its reaper executes host-side (implement-now's janitor, riding NR-2's amendment) rather than in this design, because the review lives on the host's proposal surface — but the in-flight predicate and the absent-timeout are defined *here* (§3.4) so the resource is reap-able by contract, not an orphaned husk. The deviation is the reaper's *location*, never its absence.
- **AP-13 — honored.** Each round's `reviewed_revision` makes a verdict certify the exact tree it evaluated; a stale live verdict is treated as absent (DINV-7) — the AP-13 seam the PRD's UB-6 opened, closed across the two-round model.
- **AP-21 — honored.** The payload is the frozen record downstream reads verbatim; the human report is its rendering, never a re-judgment.
- **One source of truth — honored (`#D-5`).** A proposal's review is one self-contained artifact on that proposal's own medium (DINV-9): never mirrored across media, never split across containers. Compactness (§3.5) makes this hold by construction — the artifact stays small enough to be whole; verbosity is a prompt defect fixed at the source, not a persistence workaround.
- **AP-24 — honored.** The measurement corpus is derived from the proposals and governs nothing; the calibration table is the human-authored dial, cited not inferred.
- **AP-23 — honored.** One payload per proposal+review, unique by proposal construction; parallel reviews never contend.
- **RW-3 — honored.** GitHub's lack of a native typed record is absorbed into the persistence *port's contract* — the GitHub adapter's fenced block is the port's honest realization for that medium, not a rebuilt comment-as-database workaround; a compact payload stays one artifact per proposal (DINV-9), and Carina's native typed record is the other adapter's shape (§3.2).
- **NR-1 / grammar-compatibility — honored.** codegate's `Finding` is a wire-grammar impl-review is compatible with (a compatibility test), not a struct it imports or edits.
- **RW-5 — honored.** One decision — the impl-review realization contract (schema + persistence + harvest + runtime, the choices that change together); the behavior contract (PRD), codegate's schema, the lane's classifier, and the owed lane amendment live elsewhere (§3.6).
- **Deviation:** AP-12's reaper location, as above; none other.
