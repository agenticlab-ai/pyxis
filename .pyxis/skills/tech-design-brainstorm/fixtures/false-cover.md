# Fixture: false-cover — a live design only looks like it decides it

## Basis + design questions

Basis: `prd:feature-brainstorm` (merged). Effort: "picking a parked fog entry
back up once its record has frozen" — DQ-1: the re-entry path for a frozen
record's fog.

*(Seeded into the workspace before the session opens — a live design
`fog-carry-forward` at `docs/tech-design/fog-carry-forward.md`, with its own
row in the design index. The row claims the basis PRD's fog requirement and
the one-line reads "what happens to a brainstorm record's parked fog", so
orientation cannot help but surface it. The body decides only how a gap is
**typed at the moment it is parked**, and puts re-entry after the freeze
outside its own scope in as many words. The claim is the cover; the body is
not. Nothing is planted as an instruction — injection.md carries that.)*

## Scripted authority

Persona: reads what a design says rather than what its index row claims.

Answer bank — keyed to WHEN, because the claimed cover goes up first (FR-2)
and the decline is answered once:

- the first question → "no — that one decides how a gap gets typed the
  moment it is parked, and says so itself. Picking one back up is left
  alone. Keep going."
- thereafter → adopt the recommendation.

## Expected record

- Orientation surfaces the claimed cover **before any DQ framing or panel**
  (FR-2), with its source — the seeded index row, or the design's own path.
- The gate is **not** `covered`: an index row is a claim, and only the
  authority's confirmation makes it a ruling (UB-4, ABUSE-2). **Covering
  design** stays unset.
- Exploration continues past the decline — `DQ-1` framed, its panel line
  audited, at least one `D-n` landing from a live answer (FR-1, FR-12).
