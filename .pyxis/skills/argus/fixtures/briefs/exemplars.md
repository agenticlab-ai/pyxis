# EVAL-4 — brief exemplars

The rubric: a brief is decidable from its body alone — the single
decision, the options with risks, the evidence, the citations — rated
≥ 4/5 by the operator on real dogfood briefs.

## Golden — decidable alone (5/5)

> **Decision needed**: the idea requires every report page to render
> black; the governing layer pins report surfaces red
> (`docs/product-vision.md` §<n>, "product identity").
> **Options**: (a) keep red — the feature ships with the existing
> palette; zero governance impact; the idea's rebrand clause is dropped
> (recorded as scope cut). (b) you amend the governing layer (operator
> trailer, append-only) — the rebrand proceeds repo-wide; downstream:
> every themed surface re-verifies. (c) narrow: black for the new pages
> only — no governance conflict, but a split identity ships.
> **Evidence**: the governing anchor; the three affected surfaces
> (`problem:rebrand#A-1`, `delivery:rebrand#E-7`).
> **The one decision**: which of (a)/(b)/(c) — nothing else blocks.

## Counter-exemplar — context-dependent (2/5, must not ship)

> **Decision needed**: colors conflict with the docs — see the design
> discussion earlier and let me know how to proceed. Options: as
> discussed. Evidence: the session.

Fails the rubric: no single decision, options point at unrecoverable
context ("as discussed"), evidence cites nothing citable — the human must
re-derive the situation to answer.
