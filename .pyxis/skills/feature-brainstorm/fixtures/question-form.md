# Fixture: question-form — turns legible in the operator's register

## Idea

"Pyxis should notify me when a feature's pipeline stalls — something
watching progress and telling me when nothing has moved for too long."

*(A genuinely new idea whose grilling naturally produces the turn shapes
EVAL-7 distinguishes: scope decisions with a concrete scenario to thread,
a consumer/channel decision, an open-form threshold decision, and a naming
turn whose only honest example is a typed skip.)*

## Scripted operator

Persona: terse; answers by option label whenever the turn offers labels.

Answer bank:

- on framing choice → the recommended framing, by its label.
- on what counts as a stall → "B".
- on where the notification lands → "A".
- on the stall threshold (open-form) → adopt the first sample answer.
- on the feature's name → adopt the recommendation.
- default → adopt the recommendation, by label where offered.

The bank answers by bare label on purpose: a turn whose options cannot be
answered that way fails the form.

## Expected record

- Gate verdict: any — this fixture asserts on the *transcript*, not the
  destination.
- **Transcript, every question turn** (EVAL-7 / FR-18): the form is
  present — plain-language question; one example (real with its source, or
  marked *hypothetical*) **or** the literal `no example: <reason>` line;
  enumerated options each stating its outcome **on that turn's example**
  (or one to two sample answers with their meanings on open-form turns);
  the recommendation marked; the turn answerable by bare label.
- **Transcript**: zero bare anchors — any `F-n`/`D-n`/document reference
  inside a question turn carries an inline gloss (quote a violating turn
  as evidence).
- **Transcript, the naming turn**: carries a typed example-skip — a forced
  scenario bolted onto it is the negative.
- **Transcript, every example-bearing turn**: no two options share an
  outcome on the turn's example (quote the turn otherwise).
- Record entries: unaffected by the conversational form — decisions still
  land as compact `D-n` rows (≤ 6 lines each); the examples do **not**
  bloat the record (NFR-2 spot-check on the produced record).
