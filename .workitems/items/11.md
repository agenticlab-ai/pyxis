**Plan:** implementation-verification — plan 001 (implementation-verification/001 · `docs/plans/implementation-verification/001-plan.md`) · **Design:** docs/tech-design/codegate-v1.md

## Goal

Technology-independence and the anti-gaming bar become tested properties: the identical harness binds and adjudicates three stacks, and every adversarial seed family stays permanently blocked in CI.

## Current behavior

Every check is proven on the Go fixture only; FR-16's zero-per-stack-code claim and M2's 3/3 catch bar are asserted, not demonstrated.

## Desired behavior

Two additional fixture repos (distinct non-Go stacks) carry minimal manifests and exercise the full ladder — bind, red, green, green-degraded, baseline, falsification (with state-dir provisioning), and test-delta paths — through ecosystem defaults alone; the catch suite runs all seed families (tautology; silenced test in three classes; machinery edits including the pin downgrade) paired with the legitimate-diff fixtures, on all three stacks where the seed applies, as a permanent CI job emitting EVT-4.

## Scope

- The two non-Go fixture repos with manifests, scripted verbs, and failure-identity parser entries.
- The matrix driver proving the ladder end to end per stack with zero per-stack code in `internal/verify` (inspection hook included).
- The permanent catch-suite job wiring EVT-4 results in the harness's CI.

## Out of scope

- Real second-repo onboarding — fixtures stand in by design.
- The advisory parity workflow — T11.

## Key interfaces

- Fixture-repo layout convention (manifest + scripted verbs + seeds); the matrix/catch drivers' table-driven entry points.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fixture matrix runs the full ladder on three stacks in CI, green, with an inspection note proving stack-specific behavior lives only in manifest data and ecosystem tables.
- [ ] The catch suite blocks every seed family case — including assertion-removal and the pin-downgrade machinery seed — and the paired legitimate fixtures pass, on every applicable stack.
- [ ] Rung-3 legs (sanity + revert) run on all three stacks, proving state-dir provisioning per stack.
- [ ] The suite is a required job in the harness's own CI and emits per-seed EVT-4 records.
