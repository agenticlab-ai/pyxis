### FND-1 — The evaluation context's trust provenance is unpinned: nothing binds the policy, the classifier code, or the acting step to the trusted mainline rather than the proposal's own tree

- **Severity**: blocking (CAL-2)
- **Lens**: upstream-alignment
- **Evidence**: shape:classifier interface `lane classify --proposal <id> --head <sha> --policy <path> --checks <snapshot>` — the policy is a working-tree path with no stated tree — and design:§2 "a thin platform-workflow adapter invokes it per proposal event", design:§3.5 "the injection surface is the policy file itself, which is ratification-gated" — vs — prd:argus-unattended-drive/ABUSE-2 ("anyone widens the lane's own policy through the AUTO lane… the floor erodes from inside") and ABUSE-1 ("a misbehaving or injected drive **(or any author)**"). Ratification gates what lands on *mainline*; if the workflow loads the policy from the proposal's checkout, a proposal carrying an edited policy is classified under the attacker's floor (the `docs/policy/**` self-unlock glob is itself inside the file the attacker replaced), and if the workflow builds or runs the classifier — or any step sharing the acting job — from the proposal's head, proposal-controlled code executes in the lane's context with the credential one step away. DINV-8's "an unratified version refuses to load" names no detection mechanism (ratification is a git-history property — the trailer on the changing commit), so it cannot currently do this job either.
- **Acceptance condition**: Bind provenance as design content: the classifier binary, the workflow logic, and the risk policy are always taken from the trusted mainline context, never from the evaluated proposal's tree; the acting step executes no proposal-derived code; state how "unratified" is detected at load (e.g. the loaded content's fingerprint must match the mainline's trailer-ratified lineage); add red fixtures to §3.8/A-7 — a proposal carrying a widened policy, and a proposal-modified classifier/workflow — each refused or routed HUMAN under the *mainline* policy.
- **Disposition**: applied — provenance bound as design content: new **DINV-9** (classifier binary, workflow logic, and policy from the trusted mainline context; the proposal contributes only its diff, as data; no proposal-derived code in any acting-job step), carried in §2/§3.1's new trusted-provenance paragraph/§3.5; "unratified" detection defined in §3.6 — the policy loads from its trailer-gated mainline lineage and a fingerprint off that lineage refuses to load (DINV-8's arm); both red fixtures added to §3.8 and A-7 (widened-policy-in-proposal, proposal-modified classifier/workflow — classified under the mainline policy); the shape's classifier-interface and boundary elements witness DINV-9 (Argus, decision principal, tier-2; delivery:argus-unattended-drive#E-32)
